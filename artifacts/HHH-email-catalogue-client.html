Holistic Health Hub email catalogue

Twenty live emails sent from the platform. This note is for review of who receives each message, when it is sent, and what it says. Placeholders such as pharmacy name, order reference and amounts are filled in at send time.

From: Holistic Health Hub <noreply@holistichealthhub.live>. There is no Reply-To. This mailbox is not monitored.

Pharmacy operational mail goes to the pharmacy owner account only, not every staff user. Account emails (invite, password reset, authenticator) go to the individual staff member.

Collection emails are held if they would otherwise send after 15:00 UK time or at a weekend, and go out at 09:00 on the next working day.

Patient emails (5)

Patient

You have been referred

When it sendsWhen HHH completes the eligibility review and activates the patient at the chosen pharmacy.

MessageHi [first name], You have been referred. [Pharmacy] will be your point of contact for your prescription orders. Pharmacy name, phone, email and address are included. Next step: use those details if you have questions about prescription orders.

Patient

Payment needed for your order

When it sendsWhen a Worldpay payment link is created or resent, and again as reminders at 24 hours and 48 hours if payment is still outstanding.

MessageHi [first name], Your order is ready to move forward. Complete payment below and the pharmacy can continue preparing it for you. Shows amount due, order reference, medicine / dispensing / delivery breakdown, and a Pay now link. Confirms this is a secure payment link.

Patient

Payment received

When it sendsWhen payment is recorded — Worldpay settlement or a manual payment taken in the pharmacy.

MessageHi [first name], We have received your payment and the pharmacy can now continue processing your order. Shows the amount, order reference, breakdown, and a View receipt link when a receipt is available.

Patient

Your payment has been refunded

When it sendsWhen the pharmacy confirms a completed refund.

MessageHi [first name], A refund of [amount] has been completed for order [reference]. It can take a few working days to appear on the original payment method. Pharmacy contact details are included.

Patient

Your prescription is ready to collect

When it sendsWhen packs are checked in or marked ready to collect. If only some packs are ready, the subject is “Part of your prescription is ready to collect”.

MessageHi [first name], Your prescription for order [reference] is ready to collect from [pharmacy]. Please bring photo ID. Partial: “[N] of [M] packs … are ready to collect. The remaining packs are not ready yet.” Next steps: bring photo ID; ask at the pharmacy counter if unsure where to go.

Not sent after 15:00 UK time or at weekends; held until 09:00 the next working day.
